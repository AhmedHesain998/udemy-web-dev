The Best Movies According To Ahmed

My top 3 movies of all-time.

The Pursuit of Happyness (2006)

A struggling man rebuilding his life from almost nothing. I think you'd connect with its themes of career change, learning, persistence, and building a better future.

The Social Network (2010)

The story behind Facebook's creation, mixing programming, ambition, entrepreneurship, competition, and personal sacrifice. Very aligned with your interest in tech and becoming a professional developer.

he Secret Life of Walter Mitty (2013)

A quiet man breaks out of his routine and starts pursuing a much bigger life. I’d pick it for you because you're trying different paths, learning new skills, and actively reinventing your career.
